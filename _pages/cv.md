## Research Experience

### Supervised Research Study Student

**Neurocomputational Vision Lab, University of Toronto** | Supervisor: Adrian Nestor  
_Ongoing_

**Project: The Shape of Face Space: A Manifold Approach to Neural Representation**

- Investigating the geometry and topology of face representations, focusing on how different race categories are organized in representational manifolds
- Applying Riemannian metrics and similarity-structure analyses to evaluate clustering, separability, and continuity in face spaces
- Developing and adapting computational pipelines to compare manifold structures across participant groups and datasets
- Exploring implications for multi-race face perception and the stability of neural representations in high-dimensional spaces
