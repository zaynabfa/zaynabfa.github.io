I am a Neuroscience student at the [University of Toronto](https://www.utoronto.ca/), specializing in Systems and Behavioural Neuroscience.

I am interested in perceptoin and how the brain encodes and interprets visual information. My bachelor’s thesis in the [Neurocomputational Vision Lab](https://www.utsc.utoronto.ca/people/anestor/) with Professor [Adrian Nestor](https://www.utsc.utoronto.ca/people/anestor/) uses neurally guided GAN synthesis to reconstruct human faces. By aligning EEG-derived neural activity with the latent space of models such as [StyleGAN2](https://github.com/NVlabs/stylegan2), I investigate how facial information is structured and represented in the brain. This work was presented at [CAN](https://can-acn.org/) and [CVR-CIAN](https://www.yorku.ca/events/brain-and-integrative-vision-conference/).

I also work in the [Visual Recognition Lab](https://www.utsc.utoronto.ca/~jcant/index.html) with Professor [Jonathan Cant](https://www.utsc.utoronto.ca/~jcant/), where I study the neural and behavioural mechanisms of object and scene perception. I am currently co-authoring a review on how context shapes the brain’s integration of global and local visual information.

Previously, at the [Keenan Research Centre](https://hcr-moves.com/project/st-michaels-hospital/) with Dr. [Yeni Yücel](https://lmp.utoronto.ca/faculty/yeni-yucel), I led the first in vivo quantification of cerebrospinal fluid drainage in a [NASA](https://www.nasa.gov/) microgravity model, work that received multiple poster awards and a travel grant to the [Canadian Space Health Research Symposium](https://www.canadianspacehealth.ca/symposium-2024).

I care deeply about bridging computational methods with neuroscience to advance our understanding of how the brain creates meaning from the world.
