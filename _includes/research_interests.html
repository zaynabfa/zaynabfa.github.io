Research Interests

I am currently thinking a lot about:

  • Perception and Neural Representation: How the brain encodes and organizes visual information—from faces to complex scenes—and how these representations adapt to context.
  • Computational Modeling: Using generative models and neural data (EEG, fMRI) to link brain activity with high-dimensional representational spaces.
  • Human and Artificial Intelligence: Exploring how principles of human perception can inspire more interpretable and biologically grounded AI systems.
