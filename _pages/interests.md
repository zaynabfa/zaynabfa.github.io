# Research Interests

My research interests focus on understanding the human brain and its cognitive processes:

## Primary Research Areas

### Neuroscience
**Focus:** Understanding cognitive abilities and brain functionality  
**Keywords:** cognitive neuroscience, brain function, neural pathways  
**Description:** Investigating how the brain works and how cognitive processes develop

### Applied Statistics
**Focus:** Statistical analysis and modeling in biological contexts  
**Keywords:** data analysis, statistical modeling, biostatistics  
**Description:** Using statistical methods to analyze biological and neuroscience data

### Social Issues Research
**Focus:** Investigating pressing social problems and solutions  
**Keywords:** social research, community impact, policy analysis  
**Description:** Researching social issues to inform policy and create positive change

## Academic Interests

### Biology
**Focus:** Understanding biological systems and processes  
**Description:** Studying how biological systems work and interact

### Cognitive Science
**Focus:** Understanding how the mind works  
**Description:** Exploring the intersection of neuroscience, psychology, and philosophy

## Personal Interests

When I'm not studying or working, I enjoy:

- **Reading and Writing:** I maintain an active blog where I share my thoughts and experiences
- **Music:** Listening to music and exploring different genres
- **Critical Analysis:** Critiquing and analyzing various topics and ideas
- **Conversation:** Engaging in meaningful discussions with others
- **Documentation:** Recording life experiences and insights through blogging

## Philosophy

My favorite word is "why," and I believe the brain is the single most important instrument that makes the entire human race curious and motivated to do things. This philosophy drives my interest in understanding cognitive abilities and brain functionality.

I like to talk, and I like it more knowing there is a remote possibility of someone enjoying or benefiting from what I have to say. So I document my experiences as I thread my way through life, hoping to inspire and help others along the way.
