Research Interests

I'm currently thinking a lot about:

  • Perception & Neural Representation: How the brain encodes and organizes visual information, from faces to complex scenes, and how these representations adapt to context.
  • Computational Modeling: Using generative models and neural data (EEG, fMRI) to link brain activity with high-dimensional representational spaces.
  • Bridging Human & Artificial Intelligence: Exploring how principles of human perception can inform more interpretable and biologically inspired AI systems.
